The problem

The distance between performing and leading is your courage gap.

Most leaders do not lack conviction. They lack a practice that keeps them honest about where they lead from truth — and where they perform.

How CourageLed™ works

The Courage Audit

Every Friday. Ten minutes, no more. Two questions.

Where did I lead from truth this week?

Name the moment. Name what you said. Name what it cost you to say it.

Where did I perform this week?

The time you stayed quiet. The time you said what the room wanted to hear. Do not judge it. See it.

Track it weekly. Over time, the ratio shifts — more truth, less performance. That is not inspiration. That is standard work applied to character.
