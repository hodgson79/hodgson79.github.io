The problem

Culture that does not power performance is not culture.

Every organization says its people are its greatest asset. Very few have built the infrastructure to prove it — the listening, the governance, the rhythm that turns a value statement into a result.

How PeopleActivated™ works

Diagnose · Activate · Measure

A culture system, not a culture campaign.

Diagnose

Establish where the culture actually is — with data leaders trust, not anecdotes they can dismiss.

Activate

Stand up the activation system: a governance rhythm, working teams, and visual management with problem-solving discipline.

Measure

Put the culture on a scorecard with a target, a trajectory and an owner — and review it with the same rigor as the operating plan.

Proof

60% → 72%

Culture index movement across roughly 30,000 employees at a Fortune 200 utility
