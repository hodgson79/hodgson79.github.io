The problem

Most organizations do not have a listening gap. They have an action gap.

Most organizations do not have a communication problem. They have a truth problem. They collect feedback once a year, publish a report, and wonder why participation falls. People stop talking when nothing changes.

How Voice to Value™ works

Four channels. One cycle.

Four listening channels — each with a distinct purpose, each designed to tell leadership something specific about the health of the culture.

CensusThe full picture, on a steady cadence.
PulseShort, frequent reads on what is moving.
ContinuousOpen channels for what people need to say now.
LifecycleKey points in the employee journey — from first day to last.
ListenHear it in their own words, through channels built for the purpose.
LearnFind the signal, the drivers and the few issues that matter most.
ActAssign an owner and a countermeasure — and tell people what changed.
ImproveMeasure the movement, then listen again.
