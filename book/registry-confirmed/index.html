The Courage Audit

Every Friday — ten minutes, no more — ask yourself two questions:

1. Where did I lead from truth this week?
Name the moment. Be specific. Name what you said. Name what it cost you to say it.

2. Where did I perform this week?
The time you stayed quiet. The time you said what the room wanted to hear. Do not judge yourself. Just see it.

Track it. Weekly. Over time, the ratio shifts. More truth. Less performance.
