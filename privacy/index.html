Privacy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

This site is operated by Patrick M. Hodgson Sr. This notice explains what personal information the site collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Pre-order registry and The Monday Algorithm: your email address and, if you choose to share them, your first name and preferred book format.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, organization (optional) and the message you send.

The site does not use advertising trackers and does not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Information is shared only with the service providers that operate these forms on our behalf: Formspree (form delivery) and Kit (email registry and newsletter, when enabled). Each processes the information to deliver the service.

How it is used

To confirm your place on the pre-order registry, email you when pre-orders open, send The Monday Algorithm if you subscribed, and reply to your message. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Reviewing or changing your information

Email info@authenticityalgorithm.com to review, correct or delete your information, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

Do Not Track

The site does not track visitors across other websites and does not change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals.

Changes to this notice

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
